Why we exist

There is talent everywhere. Opportunity and exposure are not spread out equally.

A student can have enormous potential and never meet quantitative finance, coding, investing, business competitions, hackathons, scholarships or leadership roles, simply because nobody around them introduced those things.

YEN exists to close that gap. We connect young people with the knowledge, people and opportunities they need to turn potential into action.

Start a business.

Learn about investing.

Get into technology.

Build your network.

Apply for internships.

Explore finance.

Good advice. But nobody shows you how to actually start.

Potential should not be determined by access.

Our vision

A future where every young person, whatever their background or access to resources, has the knowledge, confidence, connections and opportunities to explore their potential and build something meaningful.

Our mission

To equip youth with practical skills, meaningful connections and real opportunities across entrepreneurship, finance, technology and leadership.

Who YEN is for

Any young person who wants to explore entrepreneurship, business, technology, finance and leadership, with a particular focus on students who may have limited access to mentorship, networks, resources or career exposure.

  • Students with limited career exposure
  • First-generation aspiring entrepreneurs
  • Students without an established professional network
  • Youth looking for their first chance to lead
